# SkyMole backlog

Features that other mining mods have but SkyMole doesn't yet, and what each one needs.
Nothing here is shown in the menu until it actually works.

## Needs data we don't have yet
- **Navigate / quickest path to a commission**: a walkable route graph for the Dwarven Mines, Crystal Hollows and Glacite Tunnels, plus pathfinding and a line drawn in the world.
- **Commission and Emissary waypoints**: coordinates for each commission area and Emissary.
- **Crystal Hollows key-location waypoints**: Jungle Temple, Goblin Queen's Den, Mines of Divan, Lost Precursor City, Khazad-dûm.
- **King tracker**: the King rotation schedule.

## Needs reading a Hypixel menu (open the menu once, `/skymole debug`, then build)
- **HOTM**: powder cost to next level and to max, total powder spent, perk suggestions.
- **Commission milestones**: total commissions completed.

## Puzzle solvers (more work, check Hypixel rules first)
- **Metal Detector** (Mines of Divan treasure).
- **Fossil Excavator**.

## Needs prices (Hypixel's public bazaar API, no backend needed)
- **Mining profit tracker** (coins per hour).

## Needs a SkyMole server (backend) — not before SkyMole has 1,000 users
- **Mining events in other lobbies**: players' SkyMole reports events, everyone sees them.

## After publishing
- **Update checker** (Modrinth API).
